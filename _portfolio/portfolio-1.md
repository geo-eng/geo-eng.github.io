DURACHEK performs indicative, initiation-limited durability assessments of concrete mix-and-cover specifications against the prescriptive requirements of EN 206 and EN 1992-1-1 (Eurocode 2), and supplements them with deterministic service-life prediction models taken from the fib Model Code for Service Life Design framework. The application converts a worksheet of mix, cover, transport-property and environmental inputs into: (a) a prescriptive compliance verdict, (b) a predicted service life, and (c) quantified remedial recommendations.
[Link](https://geodam.ir/software/duracheck-en).
