# Selected Technical Projects & Research Themes

## Dam Engineering

Work and publications related to cemented material dams, embankment dams, upstream sealing systems, low-modulus foundations, and long-term dam behavior.

## Geotechnical Laboratory Research

Experimental investigation of soil and plastic-concrete behavior, including small-strain dynamic properties and critical hydraulic-gradient studies.

## Geotechnical Instrumentation

Application and interpretation of instrumentation data for engineering structures, including comparison of numerical predictions with measured field behavior.

## Erosion Control

Research and engineering work related to slope and channel erosion control using geosynthetic solutions such as geocells.

## Construction Materials Testing Technology

Development and application of testing equipment and methods for concrete, soil, aggregates, asphalt, and other construction materials.
