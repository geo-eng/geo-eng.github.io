Cemented Material Dams (CMDs) are a new generation of gravity dams that allows construction of a rigid dam body on almost every rock foundation. Four FSHDs (a main branch of CMDs) have been designed and now they are under construction in IRAN. Upstream face of FSHDs is a key element in designing of such dams. Three practical ways to construct this element are: (1) reinforced concrete like concrete face rockfill dams, (2) exposed PVC Geomembrane after completion of dam body and (3) covered PVC Geomembrane constructed simultaneously with body. The first method is widely used but has some disadvantageous in construction because it needs more time to complete. The second method is not applicable when protection of PVC is necessary. The third method is also a classic method used all over the world but for some foundations and heights they will have some large deformations and will not work well especially under construction. In this paper advantages and disadvantageous of all these method is stated and a new combination method that is under construction is introduced.
