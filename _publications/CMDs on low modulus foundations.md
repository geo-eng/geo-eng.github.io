Cemented Material Dams (CMDs) are a new generation of gravity dams that allows construction of Concrete dam bodies on almost every rock foundation even on very low modulus and weak rocks. Due to the weakness of the rock, more consideration is needed for a successful design. Four FSHDs (a main branch of CMDs) have been designed and now they are under construction in IRAN. All of mentioned dams are constructed on weak and very low modulus foundations. One of them (Dasht-e-Palang) is a combination of Earthfill and FSHD dam. In all these cases, For reducing tensile stresses in the dam body, Restricting dam body elasticity modulus by special consideration in mix design and source of aggregates is necessary. Watertightness of the dam has been provided by a combination of exposed PVC geomembrane and covered PVC. PVC has been used to have the ability of tolerating large deformations especially along the axis of dam. In Dasht-e-Palang dam, advantageous of symmetrical Hardfill body allows us to design a well contact between rigid body (FSHD) and Embankment. In this paper investigations of basic design and back analysis of instrumentations of these CMDs on weak rocks are presented.
