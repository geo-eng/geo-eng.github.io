The International Federation of Consulting Engineers (FIDIC) has published a guide on the interpretation and application of the ISO 9001:2015 standard for the consulting engineering industry, aiming to provide appropriate recommendations regarding the implementation of ISO 9001:2015 requirements within a quality management system. This guide seeks to assist consulting engineering firms in selecting and determining the best approach to applying the standard within their quality management systems.
