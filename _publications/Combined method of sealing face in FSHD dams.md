In recent years, a new generation of gravity dams called CMD (cemented matrial dams) has emerged in the world, including FSHD dams. These dams have major advantages such as the possibility of building a gravity dam on weak foundations, higher safety, better seismic behavior and faster implementation and environmentally compatible than other types of dams. One of the key elements of this type of dam is the sealing process, and its common method is to implement a reinforced concrete face similar to CFRD dams. Implementation of the integrated face after the completion of the construction of the dam, in addition to the difficulty and increase of the execution time, makes it impossible to operation the dam step by step. Another solution is to use the Winchester panel method, which may not be useful in weak foundations due to the large deformations of the body and foundation. Kahir Dam, the first FSHD is under construction and the dashtepalang dam, is one of the largest combined dams in Iran, with a height of more than 50 meters and a very weak rock foundation. For both dams, concrete surface and the sealing panel option were not used for the above reasons. Therefore, in this paper, new methods for the possibility of replacing the concrete face with the combined geomemberin face have been investigated. The results of numerical modeling in this study showed that it is more appropriate in weak foundation so that in cases where it is not possible to use the integrated face after construction, the sealing face should be implemented in combination.
